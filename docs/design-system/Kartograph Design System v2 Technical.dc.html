04

Rationale

Why blue, on cool slate

A precise, technical blue anchors focus and trust. It sits on cool slate neutrals and crisp high-contrast surfaces — sharp but never harsh — so the tool reads as exact and calm. Path accents (green / amber / soft red) are desaturated to live inside the same family, signalling without shouting a traffic-light.

Why these types

Hanken Grotesk is a humanist sans with open, approachable shapes — readable for non-technical stakeholders at a generous 16px+ base. JetBrains Mono gives the Gherkin and slugs a calm, evenly-spaced rhythm that's unmistakably "code" without feeling cold. Strong size and weight contrast does the hierarchy; colour stays reserved for meaning.

Two dimensions, never merged

Maturity (path pips) and progress (state dot + segmented control) use deliberately different shapes so they never blur into one bar. A feature can be fully mature yet mostly Open, or fully Accepted yet happy-only — and the screen shows both truths at once.

Accessible by construction

Every status cue pairs colour with shape or label (filled vs hollow pips, glyphs in the segmented control, written counts). Text meets WCAG-AA on every surface, focus is always visible, and the same tokens drive a full dark theme.
